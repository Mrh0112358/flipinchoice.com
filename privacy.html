Section 01

Who We Are

Flipin' Choice is a decision-making web application that uses a coin flip and psychological reaction technique to help visitors discover what they truly want. The site is operated as an independent website based in the United Kingdom.

For any privacy-related queries please contact us at: hello@flipinchoice.com

Section 02

What Information We Collect

Flipin' Choice is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide any personal information to use the service.

Options you enter: The two options you type into the coin flip tool are processed entirely within your browser in real time. These options are not sent to any server, stored, logged, or retained by us in any way.

Automatically collected data: Like most websites, certain technical information may be automatically collected when you visit, including your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and time of visit. This data is collected via third-party services described below and is used in aggregate, anonymised form only.

The right to erasure — you have the right to request that we delete any personal data we hold about you.

The right to restrict processing — you have the right to request that we limit how we use your data.
